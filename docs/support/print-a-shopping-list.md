# Print a shopping list

1. Open **Me** and choose **Printable documents & Kindle**.
2. Under **Document**, choose **Shopping list**.
3. Choose **Preview document** if you want to review it first.
4. Choose **Print / Save PDF** to open your browser's print options. If your
   browser offers a PDF destination, select it to save a copy without
   printing. You can also close the print dialog without printing or saving.

**Download CSV** in **Shop** is a separate export, not a PDF. Printing does
not email the list, record a purchase, or add anything to the pantry.
