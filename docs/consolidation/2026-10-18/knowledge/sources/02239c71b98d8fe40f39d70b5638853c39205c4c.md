## Portable contract and exact remaining owner integration

validateHealthImport(serializedJSON, trustedContext) returns a private preview
with zero writes. Envelope keys: schemaVersion offmenu-health-import/1, owner,
memberId, revision, source {kind,sourceId,exportId}, records. Limits: 64 KiB UTF-8,
100 rows. Current owner/member/revision/clock and scoped active consent are
injected by the account owner service, never trusted from an upload. Consent
must bind owner/member, purpose health-import, source, types and expiry.

Records require original id/sourceRecordId, supported type/unit, nonnegative
finite observed value, unambiguous nonfuture UTC timestamp. Units cover the
existing eight health-model types; no conversion or interval aggregation.
Unknown types/fields, missing values and duplicate identities produce row-index
review reasons without raw values. Unknown is not zero. Accepted rows retain
source/export/consent identity and are imported-unverified. Caller retains the
original private upload for review under its retention policy; rejected raw
records are not copied to telemetry or public state. Separate-source observations
must not be summed. Overlap reconciliation is not implemented by this boundary.

Existing public/health-model.js validates rows but generates ambient IDs/times
and lacks an account import envelope. This slice does not replace that path.

on private upload, require confirmation of exactly the accepted subset, recheck
grants and perform revision-checked persistence. Owner 03 supplies private UI
