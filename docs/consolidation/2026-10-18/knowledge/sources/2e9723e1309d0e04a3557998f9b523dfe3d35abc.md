New pure module supports substitute, calendar and retailer proposal records.
It preserves the supplied JSON, source identities/provenance, quantity and
uncertainty without conversion or defaulting. Distinct source/candidate IDs are
required for substitutions. Calendar intervals must be explicitly offset,
valid and increasing. Retailer destinations are opaque IDs, not purchase URLs.
Bounds: 1–100 uniquely identified records and at most 65,536 JSON characters.
Injected `now` is mandatory. No storage or transport is used or needed here.
Source derive/export eligibility delegates to existing `recordPermission`, with
reviewed policies and market injected by the owner. Consent never supplies rights.
Rights and retention are rechecked at confirmation, including after preview.

## Owner-controlled adoption contract

An integrations/UI owner may collect shoppingItems output plus the actual
rights-bearing source records, then supply a plain JSON draft with `id`, `scope`
(account AND kitchen), `revision` (current source/state revision), `kind`,
`records`, and `detail`. The owner must associate shopping lines/quantities with
source IDs in that draft; the module cannot reconstruct provenance from the
lossy ItemList. Scope must come from the authenticated active session, not from
untrusted stored data. Policies must come from reviewed policy custody.

Call `proposeShoppingHandoff(draft, {now, policies, market})`. Display the entire
proposal including destination, records, quantities, uncertainties and limits.
After an explicit user action, pass to `confirmShoppingHandoff` the CURRENT draft
and `{confirmed:true, scope, revision, proposalId, snapshot}` from that preview.
Any draft change invalidates the serialized snapshot. Confirmation produces
`confirmed-proposal` only; it neither changes pantry/calendar nor sends data.
This is local consent evidence, not an authentication token or tamper-proof audit.
Consumers must not accept a caller-forged consent object as server authorization.

UI adoption, source-record linkage, policy refresh/revocation, authenticated scope,
role checks, audit persistence, retry/idempotency (P0-05), reviewed regional offer
coverage (DATA-11), destination validation, and any external adapter remain owner
integration dependencies. Repeated confirmation is side-effect-free, not proof
of exactly-once provider delivery. No provider adapter or purchase capability is
implemented. No medical/allergen/nutrition equivalence, ownership, stock, expiry
or availability claim is inferred. Substitutions retain the original records;
confirmed proposals do not alter recipes or inventory.
