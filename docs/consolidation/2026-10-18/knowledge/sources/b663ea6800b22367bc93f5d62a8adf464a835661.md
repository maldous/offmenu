## Consumer entrypoint and ownership

The entrypoint accepts only `{packageId, lotId}`. A request cannot inject assertions, owner, rules, policies, identity matches or review verdicts. Current preferences, package, product, lot, evidence and policy snapshots come exclusively from the trusted owner loader. Consent, verified package/product identity, exact product version and consistent market/owner are mandatory. Missing actual package evidence explicitly returns unknown. Account authority and policy revocation must be checked by the real production owner at invocation and at the required atomic finalization/consumption boundary; this module adds no authentication or transaction implementation. Completion rereads cannot substitute for the real owner transaction.

Snapshot shape: `{owner, revision, policyRevision, market, package, lot, product, preferences, evidence, policies}`. Authority includes a nonempty `revision`; snapshot and policy revisions are nonempty strings. Package fields include id/owner/lotId/lotCode/productId/productVersion/market/assertionSubject/matchStatus. Lot fields include id/owner/lotCode/productId/productVersion/market. Product fields include id/owner/version/market/assertionSubject/matchStatus. The loader must provide actual package identity rather than a generic concept/product label. Preferences carry id, owner, category, sourceId, revision, recordedAt, positive explicit weight and rule. Allergy, intolerance, religion, preference and dislike are separate categories. No weights are inferred from clinical importance.

Assertions carry exact `scope:{kind:'lot',packageId,lotId,lotCode,productId,productVersion,market}`, owner, subject, market, verified match, sourceId/id, sourceRecordId, revisionId, sourceUrl, contentSha256, authorityId and validFrom/validUntil/freshUntil. Dates require explicit UTC strings and valid calendar dates, with exclusive expiry bounds. Package/certificate scope is not broadened to other lots or products. User source claims alone do not satisfy the trusted gate.

`authorizeEvidence(row,binding)` must return `allowed:true`, exact owner/sourceId/assertionId/authorityId/packageId/lotId/productVersion/market, rightsRevision, HTTPS rightsEvidenceUrl, reviewCommit equal to the injected reviewPin and reviewVerdict `approved`. This is an injected review requirement, not an approval claimed by this implementation. Existing evaluator source-policy checks still apply separately for display and derive; expired, revoked or unauthorized policy does not produce a positive rule result. Certificate-only preferences still require the existing evaluator's identified scheme/certifier check. Gate and evaluator receive frozen copied JSON; input/output snapshots remain immutable. Accessor/non-JSON inputs fail closed.

## Results and coverage

Pass means the admitted source assertions satisfy the selected rule at the evaluation time. It never means safe, medically suitable or guaranteed free of cross-contact. Unknown, conflict and fail remain explicit; automaticSelection and clinicalCertainty are always false. A supported overall fail/conflict can coexist with unknown rules, which remain visible in the per-rule results and coverage. No actual owner package assertion is bundled with this change; positive test account data is synthetic and exercises the existing evaluator, not a real certification or product assertion.

Weights are summed once per unique preference ID, not per source/evidence row. Identical duplicate preference/evidence IDs collapse; conflicting preference revisions withhold the decision. Different preference IDs with identical category/rule definitions require reconciliation and cannot inflate coverage. Colliding source/assertion IDs with inconsistent contents preserve conflict. Distinct admitted source assertions with differing values also preserve the actual evaluator's conflict. Known coverage is `(passWeight+failWeight)/totalWeight`; unknown and conflict weights have separate fractions. There is no safety score. Invalid/missing scope or provenance has no positive contribution. Clock/freshness is rechecked after the asynchronous authority gate.
