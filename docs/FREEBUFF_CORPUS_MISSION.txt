Domain-specific evidence rules
- INGREDIENTS/RECIPES: distinguish food identity, cultivar, cut, preservation, cooked/raw/prepared state, edible fraction and units. Generic flour is not automatically wheat flour. Keep instructions' ordering, safety-relevant temperature/time, quantities, yield, equipment and ingredient references consistent. Preserve cited regional recipe variants instead of averaging them into an invented global recipe. A 'best' recipe is a transparent, reproducible, evidence-weighted representative (e.g. completeness/clarity/reliable source) with alternatives; a synthesized improvement is separately labeled, cited and unvalidated until culinary/safety review. LLM preference is not independent recipe testing.
- DIETARY/NUTRITION: preserve verified/conflict/unknown states, ingredient/allergen evidence, market/date/certification authority and religious/regulatory/clinical context. Similar names, assumed ingredients or translated labels cannot certify allergy safety, halal/kosher, therapeutic suitability or safe preparation. Nutrition definitions, units and prepared-state denominators must agree before comparison. Unknown stays unknown, never zero. No child calorie-deficit or finish-food rewards.
- PRODUCER/SUPPLIER/RETAILER: merge only evidence-backed entity identities, not name similarity. Preserve corporate registration, dates, brands, facilities, markets and relationship types. GTIN prefixes do not establish origin or ownership.
- RECALLS: use authoritative dated notices, source-native notice IDs, jurisdiction/markets, product/brand, affected batch/lot/GTIN, reason/status/period and original citations. A translated global index is not proof a recall applies worldwide. Preserve conflicting amendments and withdrawal/expiry history, actionable exact-match conditions and update cadence.
- PRICES: retain currency, geography/retailer, timestamp, package size, unit basis, tax/shipping/promotion/availability and observed vs converted amounts. Compute min/average/max only for compatible explicitly defined cohorts and date windows with outlier policies/counts/source lineage. FX is dated evidence and conversion separate; never average incomparable packages, currencies or periods. LLMs validate cohort meaning; actual aggregations are deterministic.
