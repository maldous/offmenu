About this snapshot & stage definitions
Discovered
A source or reference is listed. Data or reuse rights may not be available.
Acquired
A retained finite original is bound to a receipt or collection manifest. This does not imply country completeness or reuse approval.
Normalized
A collection manifest or dated country execution index reports structured records. Units and original scope remain separate.
Gold-reviewed
Requires explicit semantic gold acceptance for the exact records. A scoped custody review or an older PASS is insufficient.
Translated
Requires explicit accepted translation evidence for those records. A multilingual source or English labels alone are insufficient.
