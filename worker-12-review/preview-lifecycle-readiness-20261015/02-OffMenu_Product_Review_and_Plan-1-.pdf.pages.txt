 Normalise once. Preserve the
 evidence.
 Normalisation, hashing, compression, embedding and distribution do not remove source rights. Physical separation helps
 governance but does not itself settle derivative-database obligations.

 Proposed data model
  Entity                            Essential fields and distinction

  Product / product version         Internal ID; original GTIN string; canonical GTIN where valid; market; brand; pack size;
                                    label version; ingredient/allergen claims; source timestamp. A retail SKU is not a
                                    universal product ID.

  Ingredient / composition          Culinary identity, synonyms by locale, raw/cooked/drained form, edible portion, nutrient
  record                            units/basis, portion or density evidence. Similar names do not prove nutritional
                                    equivalence.

  Recipe / ingredient line          Recipe version; source text; parsed amount/range/unit; optionality; preparation; yield;
                                    mapped identity; parsing confidence; reviewed state; licensed instructions/images.

  Lot / batch / serving / event     Household and location; received/opened/frozen dates;
                                    use-by/best-before/check-reminder separately; quantity basis; cook batch;
                                    served/eaten/saved/discarded events.

  Offer / observation               Retailer and store; region; date; currency; pack quantity; price basis; loyalty condition;
                                    promotion; availability evidence; delivery/minimum-order conditions.

  Source assertion /                Provider, source ID/URL, retrieval and effective times, licence/version, attribution,
  permission                        allowed uses/cache/export, transformation history, confidence and correction record.



 Ingestion and publication pipeline
 1. Register the source and approved uses. 2. Retain raw evidence only where permitted. 3. Parse and
 normalise into a source-labelled staging area. 4. Validate units, identities, duplicates and anomalies. 5.
 Send uncertain mappings to review. 6. Publish a versioned feature projection. 7. Refresh, withdraw and
 reprocess through the same auditable path.

 Storage that fits the product
 Keep licensed raw releases and large compressed snapshots in object storage where permitted. Use
 indexed operational tables for fast barcode, ingredient and household queries. Deliver small
 regional/offline packs and incremental updates. Retain commercial provider responses only within
 permitted bounds; do not dump whole third-party catalogues into every phone or user-state snapshot.

 Choose evidence by field, not one universal winner
 Use exact current package evidence for product-specific labels; reviewed regional composition for generic
 foods; explicit user corrections for that private lot. Retain conflicting assertions and show uncertainty.
 Version nutrient calculations so a later upstream correction does not silently rewrite dietary history.

 Licence release gate: complete attribution, adapted-content obligations, permitted caching/export, commercial use, image
 rights and withdrawal handling. Never mix personal health records into a public share-alike data release. OFF, AFCD and
 commercial API obligations differ. [8-16]
