Evaluate coverage by real tasks: barcode-to-product, product-to-owner, owner-to-parent, origin by field,
current claim/certificate, recall-lot identification and menu-to-ingredient traceability. Report these separately
per country and language; a provider's country presence does not establish field-level coverage.

Refresh cadence should follow each source's publication pattern and the consequence of staleness. New recall
notices and certificate suspensions deserve fast updates; annual size metrics should preserve fiscal year.
Revalidate rights and withdrawn sources as well as content. These are proposed operating decisions, not
implemented pipelines.

Food composition and vocabulary can bridge the separate food/menu discovery work: FoodData Central
contributes branded GTIN and nutrient fields; FoodOn and ChEBI help normalize food and ingredient identities.
These joins must preserve recipe version, units and market. Generic nutrients or a chemical hazard record do
not prove a particular meal's ingredients, exposure or suitability.
